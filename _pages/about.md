Hello! I'm Yeek Sheng, a software engineer based in Singapore and a Product Developer at Titansoft. I work on backend services, event-driven systems, and production reliability using .NET, Kafka, Kubernetes, and SQL Server.

My recent work includes reducing status-related transaction failures from 5–10% to under 1%, migrating 11+ projects to Kafka KRaft without downtime, and preparing a platform for peak concurrency of 100,000 users through load testing and stability improvements.

I also use Claude agents for support investigations and daily development, following a workflow of planning, test-driven development, and implementation.

I graduated from Nanyang Technological University with a Bachelor of Computing in Computer Science, Honours (Highest Distinction), and a specialization in Artificial Intelligence. My interests include distributed systems, backend engineering, and practical applications of AI.
